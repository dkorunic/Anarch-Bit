Anarch for CircuitMess Bit

The public domain Doom-style shooter, flashed straight from your browser. Version ….

  1. Use desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera (they have Web Serial; Firefox and Safari don't).
  2. Connect the Bit with a USB-C data cable and switch it on.
  3. Press Connect, pick the Bit's serial port, then Install. Leave Erase device unticked to keep your progress.
  4. When it's done, switch the Bit off and on.
Your browser can't flash devices. Open this page in desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera. Flashing needs a secure (https) page.

This replaces the Bit's stock firmware.

Updating keeps Anarch's saved progress (unlocked levels, settings) unless you tick "Erase device". A quicksave, and a game kept to resume after sleep, only load in the version that stored them.

Back to stock: flash the image from the Bit-Firmware releases. Source, controls and other ways to flash: github.com/dkorunic/Anarch-Bit.
